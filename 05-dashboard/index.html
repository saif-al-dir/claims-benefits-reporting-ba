Key Findings

📉 The mean hides the tail

Disability's average cycle time (19.8 d) sits at its 21-day SLA — but the P90 is 49 days. Reported on averages alone, the exposure is invisible.

🔧 The Disability gap is structural

All Team Delta handlers score 76.0–77.4% SLA while every other handler is ≥91.8% at comparable workload. The driver is SLA calibration and process design — not people.

💶 Small count, large money

339 overpaid claims are 0.23% of volume — but €650,236 of exposure. KPI-06 is deliberately EUR-based: a count-based control would call this negligible.

🧪 Data quality is measured, not assumed

Every load runs three gates (45 critical rejections, 40 duplicates, 261 unknown-handler defaults — all logged) and 24 CI assertions; a degenerate dataset fails the build.
